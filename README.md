## 👩‍💻 About me

I am a **Computer and Communication Engineering graduate** from Chittagong, Bangladesh (CGPA 3.758). I build end-to-end computer vision and deep learning pipelines in **Python and PyTorch**, from dataset cleaning to GPU training and evaluation.

My thesis, **BAGFD-Net**, reached **90.46% mIoU** on crack segmentation and is accepted for **oral presentation at IEEE ICISET 2026**, where I am first author. I want to apply imagery-based AI to **agriculture and field operations**.
